Feb 25, 2026 Visualizing Cambridge Open Data (Zoom)

Join us for a 90-minute workshop on turning Cambridge open data into clear, compelling charts and maps.

Session Details

  • Date: Feb 25, 2026
  • Time: 7:00 PM Eastern Time
  • Format: Zoom webinar
  • Register for the webinar Registration required to receive the access link.

What We'll Cover

Learn how to pick the right chart, avoid common visualization pitfalls, and design for clarity. We'll practice with the Cambridge Open Data Portal and Google Sheets, then share other visualization and dashboard options, including mapping tools and AI-assisted approaches.
